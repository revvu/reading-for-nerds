# Research

What the literature says about reading faster and learning from what you read, and what it means for this app.

- `reading-methods.html`: methods for faster screen reading (RSVP, phrases, listening and others), ranked by speed at equal comprehension, with live demos. Decisions D1 (default speed) and D2 (phrase mode).
- `beyond-reading.html`: how to learn from a selected passage (recall, explain-back, predicting, concept maps and others), whether richer media beat text, a test of Apple's on-device model, and a sketch of a learning key.
- `adaptive-text.md`: how a text written for everyone could adapt to what one reader already knows: compression, the right level of challenge, and the ideas that matter.

The HTML pages are built from the `*.src.html` sources, `page.css`, `demos.js` and the project skin at `.lavish-shared/lavish-skin.css`.
Rebuild them with `python3 docs/research/build.py`.
